# A10 – RC2 Sliding Fit Design

Design and fabricate a pair of parts (a hole/slot feature and a matching shaft/insert feature) that demonstrate an RC 2 sliding fit on a SLA print as defined in the Machinery’s Handbook:

> “RC 2 Sliding fits are intended for accurate location, but with greater maximum clearance than class RC 1. Parts made to this fit move and turn easily but are not intended to run freely, and in the larger sizes may seize with small temperature changes.”

## 5. Fit Test (20%)

Use digital calipers to measure:

Hole/slot diameter or width

Shaft/insert diameter or width

Compare your measurements to the RC 2 tolerance range.

Document whether the parts slide smoothly but locate accurately as intended.

Show your final parts in class for verification.

Note: Credit will not be given for parts that lack a meaningful or creative purpose. Your design must demonstrate applied understanding of an RC 2 sliding fit in a useful or innovative artifact.
