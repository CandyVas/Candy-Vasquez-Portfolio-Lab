## Research  
When choosing what infill to choose, i simply looked at PuraSLicers list of options and chose the most interesting names
i did my reserahc using this [website](https://all3dp.com/2/strongest-infill-pattern/) as well as this one
[here](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/infill-patterns_177130)

Infill percentage affect mechanical properties like how much of the interior of a print is filled. A higher infull percentage increases strength and decreses flexibility, while also increasing materials used, weiht, and print time. Whereas the infill pattern determines the geometry and direction that the infill is being built. However, not all infill patterns are built equally, some are stronger than others, as it distributes forces differently. 

<img width="170" height="208" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-03 135349" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2f09d62b-f54e-4f17-9a64-ab531a4617dc" />

> **Concentric**
<img width="170" height="124" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5405367b-fa3d-41f2-9976-49a2de409a79" />
<img width="130" height="124" alt="concentricfinal-1-2048x1536" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/600375dc-4b5f-4411-897c-02a9c91c32d4" />

> Concentric infill consists of lines that follow the shape of the object's outer perimeter. Each successive line becomes smaller toward the center. It is useful when the geometry of the part or its flexibility is more important than maximizing mechanical strength Concentric has high reported strength specifically in the Z direction, rather than being uniformly strong in every direction

> **Hilbert Curve **
<img width="130" height="124" alt="hilbert_curvefinal-2048x1536" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cbc085e4-ee4a-494d-926b-bff61211a0d4" />
<img width="130" height="124" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c8582e65-590c-4a8f-b45a-e0d4c2ec1cb7" />

>The Hilbert curve creates a rectangular labyrinth-like structure throughout the interior of the part rather than forming simple straight grids. Hilbert Curve is primarily used for  its appearance and for aestheic purposes like filling with epoxy to create art.

> **Lightning**
<img width="130" height="124" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9026ec98-ea10-4506-b935-e4c80dc7f5f3" />
<img width="130" height="124" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d15c8a1a-184a-47fe-9e79-683927be1490" />

> Lightning infill is a branching, tree-like structure. Instead of filling the entire interior uniformly, the branches become progressively denser near the upper. The primary purpose of Lightning infill is to support the top layers while using as little material as possible. Lightning is primarily designed to support top surfaces while minimizing material, rather than maximizing mechanical strength
