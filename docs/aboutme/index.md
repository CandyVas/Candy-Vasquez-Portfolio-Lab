## Call Me Candy!


My path to mechanical engineering has not been a linear one. Before settling on engineering, I explored several different fields through the many courses available to me via college. My initial interest was in interior design, which eventually led me to architecture. Civil engineering was on my radar at this point, but I was uncertain if it was something I was capable of. This aversion led me to courses adjacent to engineering, but not quite. Sustainability and energy was a lot of fun, but taking these classes opened my perspective to how engineering connects many of the subjects I had already been interested in.

When I began college at Appalachian State University, I had one idea of what I wanted to pursue, but each class introduced me to something new. Appalachian State offered sustainability as part of its general education curriculum, and through those courses, I was introduced to engineering and the way it connects with areas such as design, energy, construction, and technology. Rather than narrowing my interests, each new subject gave me another question to explore.
That curiosity ultimately led me to engineering, and more specifically, mechanical engineering. What appealed to me most was its versatility. Mechanical engineering can be applied to energy, manufacturing, product development, transportation, and many other fields, and I enjoy the idea of pursuing a field that would allow me to continue learning and exploring rather than limiting me to one area.

UNC Charlotte has also given me opportunities to see what engineering looks like in practice. One of my favorite classes was an introductory engineering course where we explored different engineering disciplines through hands-on projects. Working through these projects and seeing how each discipline approached problems helped me confirm that this is something I wanted to do.

I am now pursuing mechanical engineering, and although my interests have changed along the way, I have learned that exploration is an important part of finding the right path. Each step has helped me become more adaptable and confident in my ability to learn new things.
